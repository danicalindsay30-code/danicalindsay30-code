## ▾ About me

I'm an Electronic and Electrical Engineering MEng student at University College London (UCL) with interests in FPGA development, digital hardware design and signal processing.

My current interests include:

- FPGA and SystemVerilog RTL design
- Digital signal processing and fixed-point arithmetic
- Hardware-aware algorithm design
- Adaptive signal processing
- FPGA prototyping and verification

I enjoy working on projects that move from simulation and algorithm development towards hardware implementation and deployment on physical FPGA platforms.
